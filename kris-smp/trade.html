How to trade

Kris SMP uses AxTrade, a safe way to swap items with other players. Nothing changes hands until both of you accept.

Trade step by step

  1. Type /axtrade playername to send a trade request to another player.
  2. The other player clicks the accept message in chat, or types /axtrade accept yourname.
  3. The trade window opens for both of you. Put the items you want to give on your side.
  4. Look over what the other player is offering. If the offer changes, check it again.
  5. Click the accept button. Once you both accept, the trade completes and the items are swapped.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/axtrade <player>Sends a trade request to that player.
/axtrade accept <player>Accepts a trade request from that player.
/axtrade deny <player>Declines a trade request from that player.
/axtrade toggleTurns trade requests on or off for yourself.
/axtrade helpLists the trade commands.

Trade safely

Check before you accept

Read every item and amount on the other side. Once both of you accept, there is no undo.

Stay put

A trade can cancel if you move, interact with something, or run a command, so stay still until it is done.

Don't want requests?

Use /axtrade toggle to stop trade requests. Scamming is against the rules, so report it to staff.
