Server rules

By playing on Kris SMP you agree to follow every rule below. Not knowing a rule is not an excuse.

  1. No x-raying

    X-ray mods, x-ray texture packs, and ore finders are banned.

  2. No hacking

    Hacked clients, fly, kill aura, auto-clickers, and any other cheats are banned.

  3. No duping or exploits

    Do not use item duplication or bugs for an advantage. Report them to staff instead.

  4. No griefing or stealing

    Do not destroy, steal from, or tamper with other players' builds and chests.

  5. No scamming in trades

    Deals must be honest. Lying about an item or running off with payment is not allowed.

  6. Be respectful

    No harassment, hate speech, threats, or slurs, in chat or anywhere else.

  7. No spam or advertising

    Keep chat clean. Do not spam messages or advertise other servers.

  8. No lag machines

    Do not build anything made to crash or slow down the server.

Punishments: breaking a rule can lead to a warning, a mute, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on how serious it is. Staff decisions are final.

Kris SMP is a fan-made Minecraft server and is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.
